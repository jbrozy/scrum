# Definition of Ready

A backlog item may be pulled into a sprint only when:

1. The user value is stated (story format or an equivalent sentence).
2. Acceptance criteria are specific and testable.
3. The Developers estimated it at or below `max_story_points`.
4. Dependencies are known and listed in `Blocked-by`.
5. It can be finished within one sprint by one developer (with help).
6. No open question blocks it.
